# Open-Source Licenses

**Last Updated:** 21 June 2026

> Acknowledgements and licences for open-source software included in the GatiMitra app. Required by Apple App Store Guideline 5.6.1 and Google Play OSS attribution requirements.

The GatiMitra app would not exist without the work of thousands of open-source contributors. We list every direct open-source dependency below, with the licence under which it is distributed. The full text of each licence is available at the linked URL.

This file is generated from the app's `package.json` lockfile at each release. If you spot an omission, email **opensource@gatimitra.com**.
