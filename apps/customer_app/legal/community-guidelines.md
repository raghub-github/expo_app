# Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** 21 June 2026
**Last Updated:** 21 June 2026
**Version:** 1.0

> How to write reviews, rate partners, and engage on the GatiMitra platform — fairly and respectfully. Required by App Store Guideline 1.1.3 (user-generated content moderation) and supports IT Rules 2021 §3(1)(b).

## 1. Spirit

Reviews and ratings help fellow users decide and help partners improve. Be honest, specific, and kind.

## 2. Ratings & reviews — what to do

✅ Rate truthfully (1–5 stars).
✅ Add specifics — what was good, what wasn't.
✅ Stick to your own experience.
✅ Use your own words.
✅ Update your review if the partner addresses your issue.

## 3. Ratings & reviews — what NOT to do

❌ Fake or paid reviews.
❌ Review-bombing (multiple coordinated low ratings to harm a partner).
❌ Insults, threats, hate speech, casteist / communal / sexist language.
❌ Sharing the partner's personal details (phone, address, family).
❌ Photos of the partner without consent.
❌ Spoilers or off-topic rants.
❌ Promotional content for competitors.
❌ Unverifiable allegations of crime — instead, report via [Safety Policy](./safety-policy.md).

## 4. Photos in reviews

- Of the food / parcel / vehicle / damage: ✅ OK.
- Of the partner's face or licence plate: ❌ Without consent, not allowed.
- Of children or other passengers: ❌ Not allowed.
- Of personal identification of any kind: ❌ Not allowed.

## 5. Tips for useful reviews

- Mention the dish you tried (food).
- Mention the route (rides).
- Mention what could be improved.
- One sentence each on quality, service, and value works wonders.

## 6. Moderation

We moderate reviews using:

- Automated checks for slurs, threats, and personal data.
- Human review when an automated flag fires or a partner appeals.
- Aggregate analysis to detect coordinated patterns.

Reviews that violate this policy are removed; you are notified with the reason.

## 7. Partner appeals against unfair reviews

Partners can appeal reviews that appear to violate these guidelines (e.g., harassment, off-topic). We review within 7 days.

## 8. Anti-retaliation

Partners may **never**:

- See exactly who left a low rating.
- Refuse to accept your next ride / order because of a prior rating.
- Contact you outside the platform after the trip.

Violations lead to immediate partner suspension.

## 9. In-app chat & calls

- Communicate via the in-app masked call / chat for the trip duration only.
- After completion, contact is closed automatically.
- Abuse of the chat (harassment, spam) → reported per [Acceptable Use Policy](./acceptable-use-policy.md).

## 10. Referrals & social

- Share your referral code freely.
- Do not impersonate GatiMitra in your posts.
- Do not auto-share or mass-message via bots.
- Use the official social handles at @gatimitra for tagging.

## 11. Reporting

`Profile → Help → Report content` or report@gatimitra.com. Identify the review, ride, order, or message. We act within 36 hours per IT Rules 2021 §3(1)(d).

## 12. Updates

Material changes trigger an in-app notice. Editorial changes silent.
