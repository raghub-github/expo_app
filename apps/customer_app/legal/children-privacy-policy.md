# Children's Privacy & Use Policy

**Effective Date:** 21 June 2026
**Last Updated:** 21 June 2026
**Version:** 1.0

> How GatiMitra protects users below 18 — and which services they cannot use alone. Required by DPDPA 2023 §9, Google Play Families policy, and Motor Vehicles Aggregator Guidelines, 2020.

## 1. Age tiers

| Age | What you can use |
|---|---|
| **Under 13** | Account creation requires verifiable parental consent. We strongly recommend parents use their own account on behalf of the child. |
| **13–17** | Account allowed with parental knowledge. Food, parcel, courier — fine. Ride services restricted (see §4). |
| **18+** | Full access including all ride services. |

## 2. Verifiable parental consent (DPDPA §9)

For users below 18, processing of personal data requires verifiable consent of a parent or guardian. We use one or more of:

- Parent's mobile + OTP (linked to parent's own GatiMitra account).
- Parent's email confirmation with a follow-up phone verification.
- Parent's digital signature via DigiLocker (where available).

We re-verify consent annually.

## 3. Data minimisation for minors

For accounts identified as below 18:

- Behavioural / interest profiling — **disabled**.
- Personalised advertising — **disabled**.
- Targeted promotional notifications — **disabled** (transactional notifications still work).
- Background location tracking — **disabled** except during active rides/deliveries.
- Crash diagnostics — anonymous only.

## 4. Ride-service restrictions

Per MV Aggregator Guidelines 2020 and our [Terms §2.2](./terms-of-service.md):

| Scenario | Allowed? |
|---|---|
| Below 18, riding alone | **Not allowed.** |
| Below 18, with parent / guardian co-riding | Allowed. Guardian is the booking party. |
| 13–17, school-pickup / drop arranged by parent | Allowed only via parent's account with rider details and a notification to the parent on every trip. |
| Below 18 driving / serving as a delivery partner | Not allowed in any capacity. |

The app prompts age verification before allowing any ride booking. False age declaration → account suspension.

## 5. Food, parcel, courier services

Generally allowed without age check, BUT:

- Parental responsibility for payment.
- No purchase of age-restricted items (alcohol, tobacco) via food delivery.
- Parcel sender must be 18+; younger users may receive parcels but cannot initiate them.

## 6. Account creation by a child

If you are under 18, you should not create an account by yourself. If you have, please ask a parent or guardian to:

1. Verify the account is permitted.
2. Set spending limits if needed (`Profile → Family controls → Spending cap`).
3. Enable safety mode (turns off promotions and location-personalisation).

## 7. Reporting child accounts

If you believe an account belongs to a child without parental consent, email **safety@gatimitra.com** with the registered mobile. We verify and act within 72 hours.

| Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| Confirmed child, no parental consent | Account suspended; data deleted within 7 days |
| Confirmed child, parental consent verified | Account allowed; minor-protection features applied |
| Adult account misidentified | Restored with apology and a free wallet credit |

## 8. Parental controls

`Profile → Family controls` lets a parent (with their own GatiMitra account):

- Link a minor's account.
- Set spending caps (per day / per week).
- Block specific services (e.g., disable ride services entirely).
- Receive a copy of every order / ride / parcel notification.
- Approve large transactions in real time.
- Revoke access at any time.

## 9. Educational outreach

We publish age-appropriate safety content (rider etiquette, traffic awareness, personal-data hygiene) at https://gatimitra.com/learn.

## 10. Children's privacy rights

The same rights as adults under DPDPA — access, correction, erasure, grievance — exercised by the parent / guardian on the child's behalf.

## 11. Google Play / App Store Family

If you are a parent enrolled in Google Family Link or Apple Family Sharing, our app respects family-policy restrictions (purchase approvals, screen-time limits) imposed by your OS-level controls.

## 12. Sensitive content & advertising

For accounts of users below 18:

- No ads from alcohol, tobacco, gambling, dating, weight-loss categories.
- No "in-app purchase" prompts beyond the core flow.
- No nudges to take rides during late-night hours.

## 13. Transparency

Annual children-safety report at https://gatimitra.com/transparency — number of minor accounts, parental-consent rates, incidents, response times.

## 14. Updates

Material changes trigger an in-app notice + parental re-consent.
