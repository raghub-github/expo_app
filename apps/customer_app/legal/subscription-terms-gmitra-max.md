**Effective Date:** 21 June 2026
**Last Updated:** 21 June 2026

> The terms governing GMitra Max — our subscription programme. Required by Apple App Store Guideline 3.1.2(a) and Google Play Subscriptions policy.

## 1. What GMitra Max is

GMitra Max ("Membership") is a paid programme that gives you, for the duration of an active subscription:

- **Lower platform fees** on food, ride, and parcel orders (per-order saving disclosed at order time).
- **Free deliveries** on qualifying food orders above a minimum order value.
- **Priority customer support** (faster first response).
- **Early access** to new features and city expansions.
- **Exclusive offers** marked "Max Only".
- **Trip / ride benefits** (e.g., reduced cancellation fee on first eligible cancellation per month).
- **Other perks** added periodically (announced in-app).

Specific benefit amounts are shown at signup and can change with notice (§7).

## 3. Free trial

A free trial may be offered (typically 7 days). Trial details, including end date, are shown clearly at signup.

If you do not cancel before the trial ends, your card / wallet is charged for the first paid period automatically.

## 4. Auto-renewal

- Subscriptions **auto-renew** at the end of each period.
- You can cancel anytime from `Profile → GMitra Max → Cancel`.
- Cancellation stops the next renewal; the current period continues to its scheduled end.
- For iOS purchases, you manage / cancel via Apple Settings → [Apple ID] → Subscriptions.
- For Android purchases, you manage / cancel via Google Play → Subscriptions.

## 6. Refunds

| Scenario | Refund |

| Trial cancellation before trial ends | No charge — no refund needed |
| Cancel within 48 hours of first paid charge AND zero benefits used | Full refund on request |

These are in addition to any non-waivable rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act 2019.

## 7. Changes to plans / benefits / pricing

- We may change benefits, prices, or plan structures with **30 days' notice** by email + in-app banner.
- For price increases, the new price applies from your next renewal date — you can cancel before then.
- Material benefit reductions trigger a re-consent on next renewal.

## 8. Eligibility

- One Membership per registered account.
- Must be at least 18 years old to buy a paid plan.
- One trial per phone-number, per device, per payment method (we detect abuse).

## 9. Account & device

Membership is tied to your GatiMitra account, not a specific device. Benefits apply on every device you're signed in on.

## 10. Suspension

If we suspend your account per [Terms of Service §15](./terms-of-service.md) for serious abuse, Membership is suspended without refund. Reasonable account actions (security holds, KYC review) do not affect benefit availability.

## 11. Termination by GatiMitra

We may discontinue GMitra Max in a city or globally with 30 days' notice. In that case we pro-rate refund the unused portion to your original payment method or wallet.

## 12. Promotional offers

Promotional rates (e.g., "₹99 for the first month") apply only to the first billing cycle and revert to standard pricing thereafter. The price-shift schedule is shown at signup.

## 13. Family / multi-user sharing

Membership is not transferable. We do not offer family sharing today. Each user must purchase their own Membership.
