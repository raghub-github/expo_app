# About GatiMitra

**Last Updated:** 21 June 2026

GatiMitra is a technology-enabled mobility, food, and parcel marketplace built for Indian users. We connect customers with independent restaurants, drivers, delivery partners, and logistics providers — all on one app, with safety, fairness, and clear pricing at the core.

## Our story

Started with a single idea: every Indian city deserves a reliable, safe, transparent way to move people, food, and packages. We are building that — one city, one feature at a time, with users and partners at the centre of every decision.

## Our services

| Vertical | What we do |
|---|---|
| **Food** | Marketplace connecting users with restaurants. Restaurants set their menus, prices, and FSSAI compliance; we deliver. |
| **Cab / Auto / Bike** | Aggregator connecting riders with licensed drivers under MV Aggregator Guidelines 2020. |
| **Intercity / Rentals** | Pre-bookable long-distance rides and hourly rentals. |
| **Parcel / Courier** | Hyperlocal door-to-door package delivery. |
| **GMitra Max** | Membership programme with lower fees and priority support. |

## Our principles

1. **Safety first.** Every ride has SOS, every partner is verified, every incident is investigated.
2. **Fair pricing.** All fees disclosed before payment. No surge above legal cap. No hidden charges.
3. **Privacy by design.** Minimum data collected, never sold, always encrypted. DPDPA-compliant from day one.
4. **Anti-discrimination.** Drivers cannot refuse rides based on caste, gender, religion, disability, language, or destination.
5. **Partner welfare.** Drivers and delivery partners are independent professionals; we provide insurance, training, and grievance channels.
6. **Local compliance.** Built in India, for India — IT Act, DPDPA 2023, Consumer Protection Act, FSSAI, MV Aggregator Guidelines, RBI payment rules, and CERT-In directions.

## How we make money

| Stream | What |
|---|---|
| Commission | Per-order / per-ride commission from restaurants and drivers |
| Platform / convenience fee | Small fee per transaction from customers |
| Delivery fee | Charged for parcels |
| Surge component | Per Aggregator Guidelines, capped at 1.5× base |
| GMitra Max subscriptions | Membership revenue |
| Partner integrations | Sponsored placements (clearly labelled "Sponsored") |
