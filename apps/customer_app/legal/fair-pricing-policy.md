# Fair Pricing Policy

**Effective Date:** 21 June 2026
**Last Updated:** 21 June 2026
**Version:** 1.0

> Every fee you pay on GatiMitra — disclosed upfront, capped where the law requires, and itemised on your receipt. Aligned with Consumer Protection Act 2019, Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 §5(2), and Motor Vehicles Aggregator Guidelines 2020.

## 1. Principles

1. **No hidden charges.** Every fee is shown at the order / booking screen before you pay.
2. **No surprise post-trip fees.** What you saw at booking is what we charge (tolls / parking are added with receipts when applicable).
3. **Capped where the law caps.** Surge ≤ 1.5× base on rides; delivery surcharges follow the same logic.
4. **Itemised receipt.** Every component on every receipt.
5. **Same price across users.** No demographic-based price discrimination.
6. **Refundable on error.** Any overcharge is refunded — see [Refund Policy](./refund-cancellation-policy.md).

## 3. Where price is locked

| Stage | Locked? |
|---|---|
| Booking screen — "Confirm" tap | ✅ Locked (the price you see is what you'll pay barring tolls/parking) |
| Surge multiplier | ✅ Locked at the moment you tap Confirm |
| Discounts / coupons | ✅ Locked |
| Tolls / parking | Added with receipts |
| Waiting fees | Apply per-minute beyond included free wait |

We never re-price during your trip silently.

## 4. Same price for everyone

We do not vary pricing based on:

- Caste, religion, gender, age, language.
- Mobile model / OS version.
- Your past spending pattern, ratings, or location stickers.
- Time you opened the app vs. your friend's price for the same trip at the same moment.

If you and a friend book the **same trip at the same moment** from the same city, you'll see the **same price**.

(Promotions, however, are personalised by city, plan, and offer eligibility. These reduce — never increase — your price.)

## 5. Promotional offers

| Type | When applies |
|---|---|
| First-order coupon | New users; one-time |
| Referral credit | When a friend completes their first qualifying order |
| Cashback | Specific campaigns; terms disclosed at the offer screen |
| GMitra Max benefits | While Membership is active |
| City launch offers | First N days in a new city |
| Bank / partner offers | Per partner T&Cs, displayed at payment screen |

Discounts are applied *before* you pay; you see the final amount.

## 6. Transparency reports

Aggregate pricing transparency:

- Average ride fare by city, by hour, by vehicle category.
- Surge frequency by city.
- Average delivery fee by city.
- Number of fare disputes / outcomes.

Published quarterly at https://gatimitra.com/transparency.

## 7. Fare audits

Any fare you dispute is re-audited by:

- Comparing the trip distance against the shortest reasonable route.
- Verifying the surge multiplier at the booking timestamp.
- Verifying tolls / parking receipts.

Refund issued for any error to your wallet within 48 hours.
