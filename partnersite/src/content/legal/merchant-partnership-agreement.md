**Effective Date:** 3 July 2026
**Last Updated:** 3 July 2026

> The commercial and legal partnership between your outlet and GATIMITRA ON DEMAND SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED for listing, order fulfilment, and settlements on GatiMitra.

This Partnership Agreement is entered into between **GATIMITRA ON DEMAND SERVICES PRIVATE LIMITED** (registered at Vill-Maulanagar, Post-Majhwe Nawada, Hisua, Bihar, India) and the **Merchant** identified in your onboarding form. It should be read with our [Terms of Service](./merchant-terms.md), [Privacy Policy](./merchant-privacy-policy.md), and [Code of Conduct](./merchant-code-of-conduct.md).

If you do not agree, do not onboard or continue using the platform.

## 2. Scope of partnership

- Listing your menu, pricing, and outlet details on GatiMitra for customer discovery and ordering.
- Receiving orders through the partner portal and merchant app.
- Fulfilling orders per platform policies and preparation timelines.
- Settlements, commissions, and wallet adjustments as per active commercial terms.
- Optional onboarding benefits (e.g. photoshoot, ads credit) where applicable to your plan.

## 3. Onboarding & plan benefits

Where your selected plan includes onboarding services:

- Photoshoot services (if applicable) must be availed within **ninety (90) days** of going live.
- Photoshoot personnel availability is for up to **three (3) hours** at the scheduled slot; dishes must be ready before or upon arrival.
- Rescheduling of photoshoot is permitted **only once**.
- Ads credit (if applicable) is subject to eligibility criteria communicated by GatiMitra and must be utilised within **thirty (30) days** of going live.
- GatiMitra does not guarantee reach, engagement, or performance from plan benefits.

## 4. Onboarding fees

- Onboarding fee may be paid upfront or recovered in **five (5) equal instalments** from weekly payouts, as selected during onboarding.
- Failed upfront payments are refunded to the source account within **three (3) business days** where applicable.
- If onboarding is not completed within **fifteen (15) days** due to reasons not attributable to you, the onboarding fee may be refunded.
- Onboarding fee is separate from commission and service fees for food ordering and delivery.
- GST invoices are raised per applicable law. TDS, if required, must be deposited by you and claimed with valid TDS certificate.

## 5. Commercial terms

- Commission, convenience fees, subscriptions, and other charges apply as per your **active plan**, city, and store type.
- GatiMitra may revise commercial terms with notice as permitted in your agreement.
- Reduced onboarding fee may apply when adding a new outlet where you already operate an existing outlet on the platform.

## 6. Merchant obligations

- Maintain valid licences (including FSSAI where applicable), accurate menu, and pricing parity.
- Accept and fulfil orders per [Code of Conduct](./merchant-code-of-conduct.md).
- Keep bank and GST details current for settlements.
- Do not divert customers off-platform to avoid platform fees.

## 7. Term & termination

- This Agreement remains effective while your outlet is live on GatiMitra.
- Either party may terminate as per [Terms of Service](./merchant-terms.md) and outstanding settlement obligations.
- Delisting does not waive amounts due for completed orders or wallet debits.

## 8. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Courts at **Nawada, Bihar** shall have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to applicable consumer and grievance remedies.
