# Account & Store Deletion

**Effective Date:** 22 July 2026
**Last Updated:** 22 July 2026
**Version:** 1.0

> How a GatiMitra merchant permanently closes their outlet and deletes their partner account — what gets removed, what we must keep, and how long it takes.

This policy explains how account and store deletion works for merchant partners on **partner.gatimitra.com** and the **GatiMitra Merchant** app. It applies alongside your [Terms of Service](./merchant-terms.md) and [Privacy Policy](./merchant-privacy-policy.md).

Deleting your account is **permanent** and ends your partnership with GatiMitra. Please read the retention terms below before you request it.

## 1. How to request deletion

For your outlet's financial safety and to prevent fraudulent or accidental closures, **a merchant account cannot be deleted directly from the app or the website.** There is no self-service "delete account" button.

To permanently close your outlet and delete your account, you must **raise a closure ticket from inside the GatiMitra Merchant app, after logging in**:

1. Open the **GatiMitra Merchant** app and sign in to your partner account.
2. Go to **Profile → Help & Support → Raise a ticket** (or **Complaints**).
3. Choose the reason **"Permanent store closure & account deletion."**
4. Submit the ticket. Our team verifies that you own the account and that there are no pending orders or unsettled dues.

Requiring an in-app, logged-in request guarantees that only the genuine account owner can initiate deletion — a store or account can never be deleted by someone who does not control the login.

## 2. What deletion covers

A closure request removes you from the platform in two linked steps:

- **Store delisting** — your outlet stops appearing to customers, no new orders can be placed, and your menu, offers, and listing are taken down.
- **Account deletion** — your partner login and merchant profile are deactivated and scheduled for deletion.

Both happen together as part of the permanent-closure request; you cannot keep the account while permanently delisting the store under this flow.

## 3. Before your account can be deleted

To protect customers, delivery partners, and your own earnings, closure is completed only after:

- **All active and in-progress orders are fulfilled or cancelled** — we will not delete an account with live orders.
- **Your wallet balance and settlements are cleared** — any pending payout is released to your registered bank account, and any outstanding dues to the platform are settled.
- **Ownership is verified** — via your logged-in session and registered phone/email.

If dues or payouts are pending, we hold the deletion until they are resolved and keep you informed on the same ticket.

## 4. What data is deleted

Once closure is approved and the above conditions are met, we delete or irreversibly anonymise:

- Your merchant profile, login credentials, and contact details.
- Your store listing, menu items, photos, offers, and operating hours.
- Marketing preferences and saved app settings.
- Device and push-notification tokens tied to your account.

After deletion you will no longer be able to sign in, and your outlet will not be recoverable.

## 5. What data we must keep, and for how long

Some records cannot be deleted immediately because law and financial-compliance obligations require us to retain them. We keep the **minimum necessary** data, isolated from active use, and delete it when the retention period ends:

- **Transaction, invoice, settlement, and tax records** — retained for up to **8 years** as required under Indian tax and accounting law.
- **Records needed to resolve disputes, chargebacks, or refunds** — retained until the matter is fully closed.
- **Records we are legally required to preserve** — retained for as long as the applicable law or a lawful request requires.

Retained records are used only for legal, accounting, audit, and dispute-resolution purposes — never to market to you or to re-create your listing.

## 6. Timeline

- **Acknowledgement:** within **48 hours** of your ticket.
- **Verification & dues settlement:** typically **7–14 days**, depending on pending orders and payouts.
- **Deletion:** once verified and cleared, your account and store data are deleted within **30 days**, except the records listed in Section 5.

We update you on the same support ticket at each step.

## 7. Changing your mind

Until deletion is finalised, you can reply on your closure ticket to **cancel the request** and keep your account. After deletion is completed, the account and store cannot be restored — you would need to onboard again as a new partner.

## 8. Contact

To start a deletion request, raise a ticket in the **GatiMitra Merchant** app as described in Section 1. For questions about this policy, see [Help & Support](./merchant-help-support.md) or email **support@gatimitra.com** from your registered email address.
