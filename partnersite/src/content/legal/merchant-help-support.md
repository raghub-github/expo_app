# Help & Support

**Effective Date:** 3 July 2026
**Last Updated:** 3 July 2026
**Version:** 1.0

> How merchant partners can get help with onboarding, orders, payments, legal documents, and account issues on GatiMitra.

Our support team assists merchants using **partner.gatimitra.com** and the **GatiMitra Merchant app**. For legal documents, also see [Terms of Service](./merchant-terms.md), [Privacy Policy](./merchant-privacy-policy.md), and [Partnership Agreement](./merchant-partnership-agreement.md).

## 1. Contact channels

| Topic | Email | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| General merchant support | support@gatimitra.com | Orders, menu, portal, app, onboarding |
| Payments & settlements | support@gatimitra.com | Payouts, wallet, invoices, TDS |
| Legal & agreements | legal@gatimitra.com | Contracts, terms, compliance |
| Privacy & data | privacy@gatimitra.com | Data access, correction, grievances |
| Trust & safety | trust@gatimitra.com | Fraud, harassment, policy violations |

We aim to respond within **2–3 business days** for standard queries; urgent operational issues are prioritised during business hours.

## 2. Before you contact us

Please keep ready:

- Your **merchant ID** or registered email / phone
- **Store ID** (e.g. GMMCxxxx)
- **Order ID** or transaction reference (for payment issues)
- Screenshots or documents where relevant

## 3. Common topics

### Onboarding & partnership

- Store approval status, document re-upload, agreement copy — support@gatimitra.com
- Digital partnership form signed during onboarding is your binding agreement copy.

### Orders & operations

- Order rejections, compensation debits, prep time — see [Service Policies](./merchant-service-policies.md)
- Menu, pricing, hours — update via partner portal or merchant app

### Payments & wallet

- Ledger entries, negative balance, settlement delay — email support with store ID and date range
- Refund of platform fees — see [Service Policies](./merchant-service-policies.md) §1

### Account & access

- Login, OTP, or staff access — support@gatimitra.com
- Delisting or termination requests — support@gatimitra.com (outstanding dues must be cleared)

## 4. Grievance redressal

For privacy or data grievances under DPDPA:

- Email: grievance.officer@gatimitra.com
- Include your store details and a clear description of the issue.
